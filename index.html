Alvaro Contreras Mellado

PhD Candidate in Economics

Industrial Organization · Financial Economics

My research lies at the intersection of industrial organization and financial economics. I study how market structure and market power in banking affect financial stability and consumer welfare, and how regulation can mitigate those effects.

I am also interested in spatial competition and financial stability in emerging economies. Before beginning my PhD, I worked in Peru's Ministry of Economics and Finance and at APOYO Consultoria.

Countercyclical Capital and Reserve Requirements in a Small Open Economy

With Diego Bohorquez

Abstract

Using a small-open-economy DSGE model with nominal and financial frictions, this paper compares countercyclical capital and reserve requirements. It studies their transmission through bank spreads and balance sheets and evaluates optimal policy rules under price- and financial-stability objectives. The results indicate material gains from state-contingent macroprudential policy, while the two instruments generate distinct inflation and output responses.
